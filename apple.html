How I work

Designing with AI.
Not around it.

AI is how I work every day: to think faster, to try more ideas and to build real things instead of static screens. The decisions stay mine, and so does the responsibility for what ships.

It also ends up inside the products: assistants that answer on your own data, agents on WhatsApp, AI-generated rooms in a showroom.

Get the highlights.

Step 01 · LLMFrame the problem. Before opening a design tool I write the brief with an LLM: goals, edge cases, risks and what not to build.

Step 02 · OptionsExplore wide, cut fast. Several directions side by side, then I keep the one that holds up on a real phone, not the one that looks best in a mockup.

Step 03 · Claude CodePrototype in code. With Claude Code the design becomes a working prototype in hours: real data, real devices, a link to share.

Step 04 · TestsCheck what matters. Small automated checks on permissions, scoring and data parsing, so speed doesn't turn into bugs.

Step 05 · LiveShip and keep going. Most projects on this page are live. I keep iterating on what people actually do with them.

Most of what I design, I also build. So when something doesn't work, there's no developer to blame.
