How I workAI in the loop

Designing with AI, not around it.

AI is how I work every day: to think faster, to try more ideas and to build real things instead of static screens. The decisions stay mine, and so does the responsibility for what ships.

It also ends up inside the products: assistants that answer on your own data, agents on WhatsApp, AI-generated rooms in a showroom.

  1. Step 01LLM
    Frame the problem

    Before opening a design tool I write the brief with an LLM: goals, edge cases, risks and what not to build.

  2. Step 02Options
    Explore wide, cut fast

    Several directions side by side, then I keep the one that holds up on a real phone, not the one that looks best in a mockup.

  3. Step 03Claude Code
    Prototype in code

    With Claude Code the design becomes a working prototype in hours: real data, real devices, a link to share.

  4. Step 04Tests
    Check what matters

    Small automated checks on permissions, scoring and data parsing, so speed doesn't turn into bugs.

  5. Step 05Live
    Ship and keep going

    Most projects on this page are live. I keep iterating on what people actually do with them.
